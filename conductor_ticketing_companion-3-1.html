Design decisions — why it works

01 / Reachability

Destination cards and the primary action are large, spaced, and thumb-friendly. The key action stays in the right-side summary on desktop and is easy to reach on a phone.

02 / Signal over noise

High-contrast cream text, a single yellow action colour, short labels, visible fare totals, and a clear selected state support quick glances in bright sunlight.

03 / Fast + forgiving

Three passengers is preselected to match the core task. Fare recalculates instantly; search filters stops; large +/- controls allow correction before issuing.
